# Company AI Redesign V1

Clean public presentation layer around the proven Company AI backend.

Preserved: database, agents, central AI, Gemini conversation endpoint, Layan voice bridge, authorization and security middleware.

Rebuilt: public navigation, structured service catalog, responsive visual system, language/direction layer, service selection flow and text-chat adapter.

Rules: no secrets in UI; privileged data stays server-side; content is data; service sections are independent; Layan is an integration boundary; main stays untouched until all verification gates pass.
