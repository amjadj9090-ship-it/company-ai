# Layan Real Voice Session v8.5

Visitor opens the Company AI website → starts voice → browser captures speech → Central AI classifies and creates the operational plan → Layan receives a concise response → browser voice speaks it → Layan visual state animates during speech.

Public endpoints:
- `POST /api/voice-avatar/public-session`
- `POST /api/central-ai/public-respond`

Governance: public voice can create plans and responses but cannot execute protected finance, legal, cybersecurity or other sensitive commitments.
